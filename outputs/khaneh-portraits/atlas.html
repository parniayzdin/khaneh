خانه · HOME

A place to remember.

Khaneh brings the lives and places of Iran into a shared home for memory. This first atlas connects portraits to the cities where people died during protests, in custody, or in the surrounding violence.

The visual language draws from Persian illuminated manuscripts: lapis blue, hand-painted gardens, warm paper, and delicate gold borders. Each thread connects a portrait to a city. It does not imply a connection between people.

The archive spans different years. These selected records are a beginning, not a complete list. Each portrait includes a source; differing accounts are identified in its story.
